Real or synthetic?

Each image is a 2-D AIRS granule of stratospheric gravity waves. Some are real satellite observations. Others come from the GEOS-3km model, sampled the way AIRS sees the atmosphere, with synthetic AIRS-like instrument noise added.

This is the data our denoiser was trained on. If you can't tell them apart, the training data is realistic.

What was I looking at?

Real: AIRS/Aqua brightness-temperature perturbations from January–February 2020, with the large-scale background removed.

Synthetic: GEOS-3km temperature output sampled along the real AIRS scan geometry, with the same background removal, plus noise generated to match AIRS instrument noise in both amplitude and spatial spectrum.

Every granule here contains clearly visible gravity waves. Real and synthetic ones were drawn from the same range of wave strengths and are shown on the same colour scale, so brightness alone shouldn't give it away. A score near 2.5 out of 5 means the two are hard to tell apart, which is the goal.
